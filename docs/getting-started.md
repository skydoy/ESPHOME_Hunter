# Getting started

About 10 minutes from the box to Home Assistant. No YAML.

## You need

- A **Hunter_LS** board (version printed on the PCB: v1.0.0, v1.1.0, v1.1.1 or v2.0.0)
- A computer with **Chrome or Edge** and a USB **data** cable (USB-C on v2, micro-USB on v1)
- Your home Wi-Fi name and password (**2.4 GHz**)
- Home Assistant (the ESPHome integration is suggested automatically)

## Boards at a glance

| | **v2.0.0** (black PCB) | **v1.x** |
|---|---|---|
| ESP | Seeed XIAO ESP32-C6 | Wemos D1 mini (ESP8266) |
| Zones | up to 8 | 4 |
| USB | USB-C | micro-USB |
